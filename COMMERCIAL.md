# Commercial Licensing

lovecash is free and open source under the GNU Affero General Public
License v3.0-or-later (AGPL). For most users — performers running it
themselves, hobbyists, anyone self-hosting — the AGPL is all you need,
at no cost.

## When you might need a commercial license

The AGPL requires that if you run a modified version of lovecash as a
network service, you must make your modified source code available to
the users of that service under the AGPL. This is a problem for some
organizations. You likely need a commercial license if you want to:

- Offer lovecash (or a derivative) as a hosted/SaaS product without
  publishing your modifications under the AGPL.
- Embed lovecash in a proprietary product you distribute or operate.
- Combine lovecash with closed-source code in a way the AGPL would
  otherwise require you to open.

A commercial license removes the AGPL's source-disclosure obligations
and is available under separate, negotiated terms.

## What a commercial license does NOT change

- The open-source version remains fully AGPL and free, forever.
- You can always evaluate, self-host, and modify lovecash for your own
  use under the AGPL without any commercial license.

## How dual licensing works here

lovecash is offered under two tracks from the same codebase:

| Track | Cost | Obligation |
|-------|------|------------|
| AGPL-3.0-or-later | Free | Publish your modifications if you run it as a service |
| Commercial | Paid | No source-disclosure obligation; negotiated terms |

Because the Project's copyright is held by its maintainer (with
contributors granting the necessary rights via the
[CLA](CLA.md)), the maintainer can offer the commercial track. No one
else can — a third-party fork is bound by the AGPL.
